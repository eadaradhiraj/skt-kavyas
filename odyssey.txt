1.1 शंस मे देवि वीरं बहुभ्रमितं सुपेशलम्,
1.2 सुदूरं चिरकालं वै ट्रोयपुरस्य नाशने।
1.3 दृष्टा बहुनृणां पुर्योऽवगतास्तेन बुद्धयः

**OD1.1**  
he vANi bahuprajJam naram mahyam zaMsa.  
he vANi bahUpAyam naram mahyam kathaya.  

**OD1.2**  
saH bhRzam bhrAntaH pavitra-TroyA-vinAzAt param.  
tena kRtAt troyAyAH pavitrAyAH nagaryAH vinAzAnat pazcAt saH bhRzam bhrAntaH.  

**OD1.3**  
saH dRSTa-bahu-manuSya-nagaraH jJAta-bahu-nara-manAH.  
tena bahUnAm manuSyANAm nagarANi dRSTAni teSAm manaH ca jJAtam.  

**OD1.4**  
saH samudre svahRdaye soDha-bahu-duHkhaH api.  
tena samudre svahRdaye bahUni duHkhAni soDhAni api.  

**OD1.5**  
sva-sahacara-prANa-pratyAgamana-rakSaNodyataH.  
svakIyAn prANAn sva-sahacarANAm pratyAgamanam ca rakSitum saGgharSaH kRtaH tena.


**OD1.6**
atIva-rakSitukAmaH api saH arakSita-sahacaraH.  
bahu icchA satyapi tena sahacarAH na rakSitAH.
